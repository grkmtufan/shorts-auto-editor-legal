Shorts Auto Editor

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how Shorts Auto Editor, a Windows desktop application, handles information when you use it.

Video files and local settings

Video selection, editing, and exported files are processed and stored on your computer. The application does not send source videos to a Shorts Auto Editor-operated cloud server. Application preferences and generated outputs may remain on your computer until you remove them.

Optional third-party features

  • TikTok: If you connect TikTok and choose to post, the selected clip and the caption information needed for the post are sent to TikTok. TikTok account authorization data is stored locally in Windows-protected storage.
  • YouTube/Google: If you connect YouTube and upload, the selected clip, title, description, and upload settings are sent to Google/YouTube. Authorization data is stored locally on your computer.
  • Optional AI highlight ranking: If you enable this feature, selected video frames, local audio measurements, and a locally generated speech transcript may be sent to OpenAI for ranking. The full source video is not sent by this feature. OpenAI processes information under its own terms and privacy policy; API usage may incur charges.
  • Video link download: If you use a supported video link, the application contacts the linked platform to download the video to your computer. The platform may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address.

Credentials and retention

Third-party authorization tokens and configured API credentials are stored on your computer. TikTok credentials and tokens, and the optional OpenAI API key, use Windows-protected storage. The YouTube OAuth token is kept in the application's local user-data folder. You can disconnect TikTok in the application to remove its saved authorization token. Removing exported videos, temporary files, or other local application data is your responsibility unless the application provides a specific cleanup control.

Sharing and third-party policies

Shorts Auto Editor does not sell your personal information. When you choose to use an external provider, that provider receives the information necessary for the requested feature and handles it according to its own privacy policy. The application developer does not operate a cloud account that stores your videos.

Children and policy updates

The application is a general-purpose desktop utility and is not designed to collect information from children. This policy may be updated when application features or third-party integrations change; the effective date above will identify the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided with the application or its developer listing.
